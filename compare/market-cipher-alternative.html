Market Cipher Alternative: ILT® vs Market Cipher, Honestly Compared

Comparison · Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

Market Cipher is one of the best-known indicator packages in crypto. If you're looking at alternatives — because of the price, the learning curve, or because you can't sign up right now — this page gives you a straight comparison, including where Market Cipher is the better fit.

The short version: Market Cipher is a composite oscillator package (A, B, SR and DBSI) built around momentum waves, money flow and VWAP, with a large community. ILT® is a focused suite built to flag volatility compression before the breakout, proven by 300+ public, dated calls on X.

Note: At the time of writing, Market Cipher's own site says new signups are paused until further notice, citing upcoming changes to the TradingView platform. Check their site for the current status.

Two different philosophies

Market Cipher combines several well-known studies into one package. Its best-known part, Market Cipher B, is an oscillator that blends momentum waves, money flow, RSI and VWAP, with green and red dots for potential entries and exits. Market Cipher A overlays trend ribbons on price, and Market Cipher SR is a day-trading tool built around support/resistance and VWAP. It comes with checklists, video tutorials and a community.

ILT® takes a narrower path: it reads volatility compression, divergence and auto-drawn zones and trendlines, and prints the Diamond when they line up — a single signal that a large move is building, rather than a set of waves to interpret.
