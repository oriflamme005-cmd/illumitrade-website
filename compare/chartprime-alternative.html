ChartPrime Alternative: ILT® vs ChartPrime, Honestly Compared

Comparison · Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

ChartPrime is a well-known TradingView indicator company with a large set of premium tools and live classes. If you're comparing it with other options, here's a straight answer — including when ChartPrime is the better fit.

The short version: ChartPrime is a broad suite plus education: four core indicators with real-time signals and TP/SL levels, live strategy sessions and a community. ILT® is a focused suite built to flag compression before the breakout, with 300+ public, dated calls on X and a 7-day free trial.

Two different philosophies

ChartPrime bundles its core indicators — including Market Oracle, Market Dynamics and Prime Oscillators — into one membership. Plans include real-time signals with take-profit and stop-loss levels, live strategy sessions every week, daily market insights and a private community; the higher plan adds extra indicators and custom signals.

ILT® focuses on one job: read volatility compression, divergence and auto-drawn zones and trendlines, and print the Diamond when they line up — one clear signal on any market, backed by a public record of calls.
