What's Included

The ILT® suite. Feature by feature.

01 · Volatility

Compression zones

Measures how tight volatility is compared with its own recent history and highlights compression zones on the chart. It describes current conditions; it does not forecast direction.

02 · Momentum

Divergence detection

Automatically detects and labels bullish and bearish divergences between price and the oscillator, on any timeframe, so you don't have to scan for them by eye.

03 · Structure

Support & resistance zones

Plots channels and zones from recent price structure, so key levels are visible without drawing them by hand.

04 · Structure

Automatic trendlines

Draws trendlines from pivot structure and updates them as new bars print, which keeps the chart tidy on every timeframe.

05 · Readability

Markers & labels

Diamond markers and Bull / Bear labels show when several of the suite's conditions line up, giving you a quick visual summary to review before you do your own analysis.

06 · Workflow

Dashboard & alerts

A compact dashboard shows the current readings, and native TradingView alerts on markers, divergences and compression levels mean you don't have to watch the screen.

Indicators describe price and volatility conditions. They do not predict future prices, and no marker or label is a recommendation to buy or sell.

Access is granted to your TradingView username usually within a few hours, 24h maximum. You'll get a confirmation once it's live, plus a quick-start guide.

Every asset TradingView offers — Bitcoin and crypto, forex pairs, stocks, indices, gold, silver, oil — and every timeframe. Many members prefer higher timeframes (4H, 12H, Daily) for a cleaner read.
